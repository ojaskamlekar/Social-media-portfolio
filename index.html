The business challenge

Reach relevant gamers and move interest into customer conversations while managing advertising costs and service delivery.

The work I owned

Instagram post boosting, engagement and message campaigns, interest and location targeting, customer acquisition, retention, and conversion optimisation.

Audience decisions

Focused on adults aged 18–35, gaming and PC-gaming interests, and selected Indian cities. Also tested audiences in Sri Lanka and Australia.

Business outcome

Approximately ₹6.5 lakh total revenue and 1,000+ customers served, with approximately ₹1.5 lakh cumulative advertising spend.

The original Instagram account was subsequently suspended. The former audience was approximately 2,600 followers. Business totals are founder-reported; no paid-attribution or ROAS claim is made.
